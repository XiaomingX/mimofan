# Math Reasoning Test Prompts

## Arithmetic

### Basic
What is 123 + 456? Reply with just the number.

### Intermediate
What is 17 * 23? Reply with just the number.

### Advanced
What is 1234 * 5678? Reply with just the number.

## Word Problems

### Simple
If you have 10 apples and give away 3, how many do you have left? Reply with just the number.

### Rate
A car travels 180km in 3 hours. What is its speed in km/h? Reply with just the number.

### Percentage
What is 15% of 200? Reply with just the number.

## Algebra

### Linear
Solve for x: 3x + 7 = 22. Reply with just the number.

### Quadratic
What are the solutions to x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0? Reply with the numbers separated by comma.

### System
Solve the system: x + y = 10, x - y = 4. Reply with x and y separated by comma.

## Geometry

### Area
What is the area of a circle with radius 5? Use pi = 3.14 and round to 2 decimal places.

### Volume
What is the volume of a cube with side length 4? Reply with just the number.

### Triangle
What is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs 3 and 4? Reply with just the number.

## Logic

### Sequence
What comes next in the sequence: 2, 4, 8, 16, ? Reply with just the number.

### Pattern
What comes next: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ? Reply with just the number.

### Combinatorics
How many ways can you choose 2 items from a set of 5? Reply with just the number.
